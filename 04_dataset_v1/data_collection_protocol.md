# Photographic Data Collection Protocol

Detailed guidance for capturing genuine photographs of the Rhino Motion RMCS-610 digital speed controller enclosure.

---

## 1. Perspectives Matrix (Minimum 50–100 photographs recommended)
- **Direct Orthogonal Angles (20% of dataset):**
  - Front view (facing terminal block opening)
  - Rear view (solid back plate)
  - Left side view
  - Right side view
  - Top plan view (showing screw recesses and vent slots)
- **Oblique Perspectives (50% of dataset):**
  - 3/4 isometric views from 4 quadrants at eye-level ($30^\circ$)
  - Elevated high-angle shots ($45^\circ$ to $60^\circ$)
  - Low-angle elevation views ($10^\circ$ to $20^\circ$)
- **Distance Variation (20% of dataset):**
  - Close-up ($15\text{–}30\text{ cm}$): Occupies $> 60\%$ of frame area
  - Medium range ($40\text{–}70\text{ cm}$): Occupies $20\text{–}50\%$ of frame area
  - Far field ($80\text{–}150\text{ cm}$): Occupies $< 20\%$ of frame area
- **Challenging Conditions (10% of dataset):**
  - Partial hand occlusion during handling
  - Low-light exposure and harsh glare/specular reflections

## 2. Important Rules
- The physical unit must be fully assembled (lid + base together).
- Do NOT annotate internal circuits.
- Do NOT use 3D CAD screenshots or renders as training photos.
